The relation uses the signature's two points only through `[s]P` and `[s]Q`, `s` the challenge scalar: `lift` multiplies both by `s` before the pairing and nothing later reads the points themselves. So for a point `U` of odd order, `P + U` is the honest `P` to everything after the order test exactly when `[s]U` is zero, which is when the order of `U` divides `s`. Otherwise `[s](P + U)` is not in the `2^e`-torsion and the pairing identity fails.
